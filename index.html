Model results are not real-world drag predictions. The physical rider-and-bike proxies are still coarse voxels. Boundary layers, rotating wheels, pedalling and realistic cycling turbulence are unresolved. Percentage comparisons stay hidden during startup or after conditions change; even settled results are unvalidated model values. We do not force the last rider to have the lowest drag.
What was corrected in the physics audit?

The old code used a very low Reynolds number, a stationary road in the rider frame, uniform flow forced at the outlet and an arbitrary 300-step settling threshold. This revision uses separate solver states, moving-ground bounce-back, two-relaxation-time collision, non-equilibrium open-boundary extrapolation, physical geometry dimensions and a flow-through plus force-stability check.
